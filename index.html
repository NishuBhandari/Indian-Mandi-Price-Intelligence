Why Do Extreme Price Spreads Occur?

Investigating price variations from Phase 2 exploratory audit

1. Quality & Moisture Discrepancy
Cash crops like Arecanut and Dry Chillies trade across raw, medium, and premium export grades within the exact same APMC on the same day.
2. Highly Perishable Horticultural Crops
Produce like Tomato, Cauliflower, and Onion experience steep price drops in evening auctions as vendors liquidate stock before spoilage.
3. Local APMC Trading Friction
Remote mandis (e.g. in hilly regions of Jammu & Kashmir and North East) experience high transport overhead and supply gluts compared to port-accessible mandis.
